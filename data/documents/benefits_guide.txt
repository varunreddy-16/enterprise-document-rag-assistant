NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES — BENEFITS GUIDE
Synthetic demonstration document — Plan Year 2026

Learning allowance: Eligible employees receive an annual professional learning allowance of INR 40,000 for approved certifications, courses, workshops, and conferences.
Wellness: Employees can claim up to INR 12,000 per plan year for eligible wellness activities.
Travel: Business travel must use the approved travel channel unless an exception is authorized. Claims should be submitted within 15 calendar days after the trip.
Expense approval: Individual expense claims above INR 20,000 require manager approval. Claims above INR 100,000 require Finance approval in addition to manager approval.
