NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Synthetic demonstration document — Version 3.2

Working hours: Standard hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM Monday through Friday.
Leave: Employees receive 18 days of annual paid leave. Requests longer than five consecutive working days should be submitted at least ten business days in advance.
Parental leave: Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave following birth or adoption. Employees should coordinate the start date with People Operations.
Remote work: Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role permits and their manager approves.
Learning: Employees may request reimbursement for approved professional courses and conferences through Learning & Development.
