NORTHSTAR TECHNOLOGIES — PROCUREMENT POLICY
Synthetic demonstration document — Version 2.7

Standard purchases: Business purchases should use an approved supplier. Requests must include business justification and cost center.
Approval thresholds: Purchases up to INR 25,000 require manager approval. Purchases above INR 25,000 and up to INR 250,000 require department-head approval. Purchases above INR 250,000 require department-head approval plus Procurement review.
Software purchases: New software subscriptions require security and data-handling review before purchase. Software accessing confidential information requires Security review.
Vendor onboarding: New vendors must complete the vendor onboarding questionnaire before a purchase order is issued.
